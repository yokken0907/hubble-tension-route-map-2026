# Manuscript Readability Policy v1.6.2

## Purpose

The manuscript should remain readable for beginners while avoiding oversimplified or false conclusions.

## Policy

- Keep chapter claims short and bounded.
- Do not treat one measurement as a final verdict.
- Separate direct observation from model-dependent inference.
- Preserve source anchors for numerical values.
- Keep Japanese and English editions aligned in claim boundary, even when wording differs.

## PDF status

Japanese and English PDFs are included and regenerated for v1.6.2.
