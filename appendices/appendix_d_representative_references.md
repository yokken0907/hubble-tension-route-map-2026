### Planck2018Parameters

Planck Collaboration. (2020).  
*Planck 2018 results. VI. Cosmological parameters.*  
Astronomy & Astrophysics, 641, A6.  
arXiv:1807.06209. DOI:10.1051/0004-6361/201833910.
