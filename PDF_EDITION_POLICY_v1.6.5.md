# PDF Edition Policy v1.6.5

This repository preserves two PDF layouts because they serve different reader needs.

## Recommended reader edition

Use the files in:

```text
pdf/reader_edition/
```

The v1.6.5 reader PDFs are regenerated from the refreshed public-final source text using ASCII-only internal anchors for table-of-contents links. They are the primary public-facing files for ordinary screen reading and learning.

## Compact print edition

Use the files in:

```text
pdf/compact_print_edition/
```

The compact print PDFs preserve the denser v1.6.2 layout lineage for printing, quick reference, and archival comparison. They are secondary files, not the recommended reader-facing edition.

## Claim boundary

Both editions are educational route maps. They do not claim to solve the Hubble tension, propose a new cosmological model, overturn Lambda-CDM, or provide expert-reviewed conclusions.
