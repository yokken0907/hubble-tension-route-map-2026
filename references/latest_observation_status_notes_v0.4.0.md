# Latest Observation Status Notes v1.6.2

## Purpose

This file records the latest-observation status used for the public-review-candidate draft.

It is not a substitute for a final release-time literature search.

## DESI DR2 BAO

Anchor:
- `DESI2025DR2BAO`

Safe status:
- DESI DR2 BAO is a major BAO update based on three years of DESI operation and more than 14 million galaxies and quasars.
- It strongly updates the distance-redshift and dark-energy constraint map.
- It must not be described as a standalone direct measurement of local H0 or a final solution to the Hubble tension.

## JWST Cepheid checks

Anchor:
- `Riess2024JWSTCepheids`

Safe status:
- JWST observations strongly test whether HST Cepheid photometry crowding explains the tension.
- The cited work reports no significant mean distance difference between HST and JWST Cepheid measurements in the sample and rejects unrecognized crowding as the explanation in that analysis.
- This strengthens distance-ladder robustness but does not identify the physical cause of the full Hubble tension.

## JAGB

Anchor:
- `Lee2024JAGB`

Safe status:
- JAGB is an independent distance indicator based on carbon-rich asymptotic giant branch stars in the J band.
- It is useful as an independent check on Cepheid and TRGB distance scales.
- It remains part of an evolving distance-ladder landscape and should not be overclaimed as final.

## S8 / weak lensing

Anchors:
- `Asgari2021KiDS1000`
- `Abbott2022DESY3`

Safe status:
- Weak-lensing surveys provide important low-redshift structure-growth constraints.
- S8 constraints depend on survey systematics, intrinsic alignments, baryonic effects, photo-z calibration, scale cuts, and modeling choices.
- S8 must be treated as an important compatibility test for H0-oriented solutions, not as a direct H0 measurement.

## Standard sirens

Anchor:
- `GW170817StandardSiren`

Safe status:
- Standard sirens are independent gravitational-wave distance probes.
- GW170817 demonstrated the method but had relatively large uncertainty compared with the CMB/distance-ladder H0 tension.
- Standard sirens are important future arbiters but not yet the sole final resolution in this handbook.
