## Required wording constraints

### Planck / CMB

Use:

> Planck 2018 under base Lambda-CDM infers H0 around 67.4 ± 0.5 km/s/Mpc.

Do not use:

> Planck directly measures the local H0.

### SH0ES

Use:

> SH0ES provides a late-universe distance-ladder measurement.

Do not use:

> SH0ES by itself identifies the physical cause of the tension.

### DESI / BAO

Use:

> DESI BAO constrains the distance-redshift relation relative to the sound horizon.

Do not use:

> DESI directly solves the Hubble tension.

### EDE

Use:

> Early Dark Energy is a major proposed route.

Do not use:

> Early Dark Energy is the answer.
