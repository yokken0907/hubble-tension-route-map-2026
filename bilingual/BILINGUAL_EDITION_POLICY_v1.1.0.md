# Bilingual Edition Policy v1.6.2

## Purpose

This repository provides Japanese and English educational editions of the same Hubble tension route map.

## Policy

The English and Japanese editions are aligned in claim boundary, chapter structure, and scientific intent, but they are not strict word-for-word translations.

- English edition: concise international educational reference.
- Japanese edition: Japanese reader-facing explanation with explicit claim-boundary language.

## Absolute claim boundary

Both editions must preserve:

```text
not a Hubble tension solution
not a new cosmological model
not an expert-reviewed conclusion
not a discovery report
annual update required
```

## PDF policy

PDFs are reader-facing artifacts. Both language PDFs must be rendered and checked before public release.
