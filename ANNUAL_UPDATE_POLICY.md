# Annual Update Policy v1.6.2

## Purpose

The Hubble tension literature changes quickly. This educational handbook should not be treated as permanently current without review.

## Annual update rule

At least once per annual edition, check:

- Planck/CMB baseline references and any major new CMB constraints;
- SH0ES/Cepheid distance-ladder updates;
- TRGB/JAGB/CCHP independent-distance updates;
- DESI/BAO and supernova-combination updates;
- standard-siren developments;
- modified-gravity and early-universe review updates;
- whether any claim-boundary wording has become too strong or stale.

## Release rule

No future annual edition should loosen the claim boundary unless expert review and source-level justification are added.
